# 8 Stylish Studded Shoes to Step into the Fashion Limelight

Studded shoes have been doing the fashion rounds for a while now, proving that a little hardware can instantly elevate any outfit into the fashion limelight. This season, there’s a studded shoe to suit almost all tastes and wardrobe aesthetics. Whether you’re after a sky-high pair of statement heels or a casual everyday sneaker, there seem to be studded versions everywhere you look. If you are looking to refresh your footwear collection with an edgy touch, take a look at these stylish studded shoes below.

## 1. Stud Pointed Shoes

![Stud Pointed Shoes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/1_stud-pointed-shoes_600x336.jpg)

Pointed shoes are oh-so-stylish right now and made even better with subtle studded embellishments. Everyone from high-end designers like [Valentino](https://www.valentino.com) to high-street stores like Topshop are jumping on board the studded shoes bandwagon. For a little over $50, you can get your hands on a sleek pair of studded shoes from Topshop. A sharp pointed toe combined with metallic studs creates an effortlessly polished silhouette that works from day to night.

## 2. Studded Loafers

![Studded Loafers](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/2_studded-loafers_600x250.jpg)

The love affair with loafers continues, so this season why don’t you go for an all-over studded finish? Studded embellishments help give the humble loafer a more daring, fashion-forward edge. While you’ll have to fork out some serious dough for designer versions from Valentino or Jimmy Choo, you can find much more affordable options at stores like [Forever 21](https://www.forever21.com), Modcloth, and Topshop. They add just the right amount of attitude to preppy tailored trousers or classic denim.

## 3. Studded Ankle Boots

![Studded Ankle Boots](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/3_studded-ankle-boots_600x378.jpg)

Studded ankle boots come and go each season, remaining a staple for cool-weather styling. Right now, opt for a Western-style boot with some subtle stud detailing along the seams or straps. Don’t worry if you don’t have the cash to splurge on a luxury designer pair by Chloé; there are plenty of chic and budget-friendly options available from brands like [ASOS](https://www.asos.com) and Topshop. Pair them with flowy dresses or leather jackets for an iconic rocker-chic vibe.

## 4. Studded Sneakers

Stay comfy this season in a pair of studded sneakers. They’re casual and cool, but the stud detailing says you mean business. Team your studded sneakers with skinny jeans and a simple sweater for a fuss-free weekend look. There’s a studded sneaker to suit any budget, ranging from high-end Miu Miu versions to accessible designs from ASOS and Forever 21. For example, a stylish studded shoe in sneaker form will set you back around $30 at Forever 21, making it an easy weekend update.

Key ways to style casual studded sneakers:

- Pair with distressed denim and an oversized blazer

- Combine with a slip dress for an unexpected edgy contrast

- Wear with monochrome athleisure for elevated off-duty style

## 5. Studded Oxfords

![Studded Oxfords](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/5_studded-oxfords_320x208.jpg)

Make your preppy oxfords a little bit more badass with the inclusion of some studded detailing. Check out classic footwear brands like Bass and Church’s for a smart pair of these studded lace-up shoes. They’re perfect for wearing around campus or on a stroll downtown when you want structure without sacrificing personality. If you prefer off-duty comfort over structured oxfords, you can also check out [studded sneakers](#4) for a more relaxed option.

## 6. Studded Heels

![Studded Heels](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/6_studded-heels_600x535.jpg)

If you’re after a true statement shoe, check out footwear with ornately embellished heels. From the front they may look like a simple sandal or pump, but from the back they’re a right riot! Look to brands like [Sam Edelman](https://www.samedelman.com), Steve Madden, and Aldo for creative takes on this style of studded shoe. Teamed with a simple dress and matching accessories, they step right into the evening limelight for a fresh party look.

## 7. Gem Studded Shoes

![Gem Studded Shoes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/shoes/2012/12/7_gem-studded-shoes_600x632.jpg)

If metal spikes and hardware studs feel too heavy for your taste, try some gem studs instead. Thanks to high-fashion designers like Dolce & Gabbana, sparkling gem embellishments are everywhere this season. Topshop and ASOS offer several styles of gem studded shoes in the $50-$100 price range. For something even more affordable, you can go DIY and glue colorful rhinestones onto a pair of plain pumps or sleek [pointed shoes](#1).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Studded Loafers | Preppy Edgy | Work & Office |
| Studded Ankle Boots | Western Rocker | Fall Casual |
| Studded Heels | Glam Party | Night Out |

## 8. Studded Ballet Pumps

When it comes to everyday footwear, ballet pumps are timeless. However, if you want something a bit more modern and on-trend, then a pair of studded ballet pumps may just be for you. Simply switch your old plain pair for stylish studded embellishments. Much like classic [studded loafers](#2), they’re easy to add to your capsule wardrobe, with plenty of budget-friendly buys available at Forever 21, ASOS, and Topshop.

As you can see, there are all sorts of studded shoes available this season to elevate your wardrobe. From everyday flats to party-ready heels, there’s a type of studded shoe for almost every occasion. Which of these studded shoes will you be stepping out in this season?
