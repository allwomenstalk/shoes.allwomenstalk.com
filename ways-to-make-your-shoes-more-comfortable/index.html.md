# How to Make Your Shoes More Comfortable: 7 Proven Tips for All\-Day Comfort

If you want to know how to make shoes more comfortable, you can save yourself a lot of pain and heartache down the road. Whether you are breaking in stiff leather boots, trying to figure out how to make loafers more comfortable for work, or wondering how to make new shoes comfortable before a big night out, wearing stylish footwear shouldn’t mean suffering through sore feet. Do you have to give up style for comfort? You can have both when you have a few practical tricks up your sleeve. Learn how to make your shoes more comfortable with these handy, proven tips.

## 1. Get the Right Fit

Make sure your shoes are comfortable from the very start by getting them properly fitted. The right fit ensures your feet are secured, preventing unwanted sliding or painful pinch points. When shopping for work shoes, loafers, or heels, try them on later in the day when your feet are slightly expanded for the most accurate sizing. However, even well-fitted shoes can cause friction after hours on your feet, so the following tips will help you figure out what to put in shoes to make them comfortable for long wear.

## 2. Wear Them in

Get used to a new pair of shoes by wearing them in around the house before your big debut. Practicing beforehand allows the upper material to soften and naturally contour to your feet. If your shoes are a bit tight, wear them with a thick pair of socks for short periods to stretch them gently. Wearing your shoes beforehand also lets you spot potential pressure areas early and decide whether to [stuff them](#3) or add protective padding before heading out in public.

## 3. Stuff It

You don’t always have to wear your shoes to break them in. Another easy trick to stretch tight spots—especially when figuring out how to make loafers comfortable or softening rigid leather—is to stuff them with rolled-up socks, shoe stretchers, or tissue paper. Leave them stuffed overnight so the material expands smoothly. It saves your feet from friction while breaking in tough new pairs without unnecessary discomfort.

## 4. Full Insoles

One of the most common causes of footwear discomfort is foot slippage inside the shoe. When your feet slide around, constant rubbing creates friction that leads to painful blisters or broken skin. A great solution for how to make work shoes more comfortable is adding a full cushion insole. Leading brands like [Dr. Scholl's](https://www.drscholls.com) manufacture full-length insoles that absorb impact across the entire sole and keep your foot centered. If pressure concentrates under the front of your foot, consider pairing insoles with targeted [cushion inserts](#5).

## 5. Cushion Inserts

Cushion inserts are another popular shoe aid when deciding what can I put in my shoes to make them more comfortable. Gel or foam pads placed under the ball of your foot work wonders for high heels by redistributing weight away from high-pressure zones. Specialty brands like [Foot Petals](https://www.footpetals.com) offer slim gel pads that take the sting out of standing or dancing for hours. Inserts designed specifically for the back of the heel also stop feet from slipping out of loose flats.

Here are quick items to check before wearing new shoes for extended periods:

- Apply friction-defense balm to high-rub areas like heels and pinky toes.
- Ensure at least a quarter-inch of clearance in the toe box for comfortable flexing.
- Test new footwear indoors on carpeted surfaces for at least 30 minutes first.

## 6. Arch Support

Sometimes wearing flat shoes might seem like a safer or more comfortable choice than high heels. However, if your flat shoes lack adequate arch support, they can cause foot fatigue and heel strain over time. Learning how to make flats comfortable often comes down to adding structured support inside the shoe. When buying new pairs, look for built-in padded soles, or try adding discrete arch support inserts to make flat shoes comfy for all-day walking.

## 7. Band Aids

It never hurts to carry a few gel plasters or [Band-Aid](https://www.band-aid.com) blister cushions in your handbag for unexpected emergencies. They are a true lifesaver if you get caught out with a painful shoe dilemma while away from home. Blister plasters cushion tender spots, ease friction immediately, and work alongside [properly fitted shoes](#1) to keep minor rubbing from ruining your day.

These are just some of the best tips to help you make your shoes more comfortable. Getting the right fit is essential, but having breakdown hacks and cushioning strategies ready will help ease pain whenever trouble arises.
