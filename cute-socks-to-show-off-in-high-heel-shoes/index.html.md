# 7 Cute Socks to Wear with High Heel Shoes for a Chic Look

Socks for high heel shoes come in all sorts of chic designs. While the heels with socks look can be divisive, if you’re a fan of the viral heels with socks aesthetic, you’ll know that half of the styling success lies in choosing the right combination of socks and high heel shoes. Whether you are looking for the best socks for high heels or styling casual ankle socks with heels, choosing complementary textures and cuts makes all the difference. Since the look leans charmingly romantic and fashion-forward, opt for cute styles of socks that work well with Mary Jane pumps, court shoes, and [heeled sandals](#5). Take a look at a few cute socks to wear with heels below.

- **Contrast Textures:** Pair delicate lace or sheer nylon with polished leather or suede heels.
- **Proportion Matters:** Ankle-length socks sit best with ankle-strap pumps and court shoes.
- **Color Harmony:** Choose neutral shades for classic chic, or bold patterns for a statement look.
